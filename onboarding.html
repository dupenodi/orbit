Welcome to Orbit

Your everyday tools, one flick away.

Hold a shortcut, flick toward what you need, let go. Orbit keeps eight everyday tools right under your cursor, in every app.

Your wheel

Eight small jobs, always within reach.

Each slice does something you’d otherwise dig through menus for. Swap any of them in wheels.json.

    Permissions

    A few permissions.

    macOS asks before any app can see your screen or press keys for you. Orbit only uses them when you pick an action that needs them.

    Everything stays on your Mac.

      Allowed Screen Recording? macOS applies it after Orbit restarts.

      Try it

      Hold. Flick. Release.

      1. Hold the shortcut. The wheel opens under your pointer.
      2. Flick toward a slice.
      3. Release to run it, or let go in the middle to cancel.

      All set
